# Your first asset, from receipt to download

The fun part is opening your new asset in a project. Let us get the receipt-to-download part out of the way first.

For this walkthrough, imagine buying **Moon Jacket** from a creator whose store is connected to Orbiters. The name is fictional; use your own asset and real purchase key as you follow along.

## Before you paste anything

Sign in to the Orbiters account you want to keep using. Keep your receipt nearby and check the creator's name. If you already use Discord to sign in, do not start a separate Telegram account just for this purchase: [connect another provider from your existing account](/documentation/orbiters.how-to.account-and-discord-login).

If your seller supplies a license key, use that exact key. An order number and a license key are not interchangeable just because both look like a long string.

## 1 · Find the item you bought

Open the asset page and compare its name and creator with your receipt. You are looking for a match, not merely a similar avatar or accessory.

## 2 · Turn the key into access

Paste the license key into the redemption field and submit it. If Orbiters asks which creator sold the product, select the seller from your receipt and continue. Wait for the result before submitting again.

> **Small discovery**
>
> A creator-selection prompt is asking for missing context. It does not automatically mean your purchase is invalid. Keys from different stores can look similar.

## 3 · Pick an available release

Open the asset's versions and choose a release your account can use. Public access does not include beta or alpha by default. If only a test release is locked, use the public release and ask the creator whether the test group is open.

## 4 · Look for the right finish line

You are done when the asset is attached to your account and its permitted download or install action is available. A Discord role can arrive afterward through the background queue. Do not redeem again merely because the role is a little behind.

## Your next five minutes

Explore [which versions your account can use](/documentation/orbiters.how-to.assets-and-downloads), or take a compatible asset into [MCB and Unity](/documentation/orbiters.how-to.mcb-and-unity-tools).

If redemption fails, use the [result-by-result guide](/documentation/orbiters.how-to.redeem-license-key). Keep the seller, asset and exact message when asking for help. Send a full key only through an appropriate private support channel.
