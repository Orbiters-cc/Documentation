# Open avatar tools and your public profile

These shortcuts require the matching website release.

Choose **Tools** in the navbar to open the ReFit and MyCustomBase (MCB) cards.
Each card opens its existing product page, which starts with a short explanation
of what the tool does. Their direct addresses, `/refit` and
`/my-custom-base`, remain available.

To see your profile as a visitor-facing page, click your navbar avatar and choose
**Public profile**. The button includes your avatar. Creators have the same shortcut
at the top of **Creator → Overview**. It opens `/user/<your Orbiters user ID>`.

## Change your profile picture and banner

On your own public profile, round buttons appear when you point at your banner or
profile picture; on touch screens they are always shown. Visitors never see them.

- The image button on the banner uploads a new banner. The reset button next to
  it goes back to your Discord banner.
- The camera button on your profile picture uploads a new picture (a still PNG,
  JPEG or WebP up to 5 MB). It is cropped to a square and shown on your profile,
  in the navbar, next to your comments and on your public cards. The reset button
  brings back the picture of your connected Discord, GitHub or Telegram account.

Discord itself, and messages the Orbiters bot posts there, keep showing your
Discord avatar.

## VPM widget on creator profiles

When a creator publishes a VPM listing, their profile gets a **VPM** widget with
the listing's banner, logo and package count, an **Add to Creator Companion**
button and a link to its public page. It is added once:
if you remove it from your profile layout, it does not come back. See
**Publish and manage a VPM listing** for the listing itself.

## Navigation on phones and tablets

On phones and tablets, open the menu button. Your name at the top opens the same
profile page, the gear opens **My account** and the exit icon logs you out. The
menu groups pages into **Browse**, **Workspace** and **Resources** and highlights the
page you are on. Notifications keep their own bell beside the menu button.

Blog articles display their saved cover image above the article text. The cover
does not need to be inserted again into the article's Markdown.

Custom-base assets belong to **MCB** and are excluded from both the **My Avatar**
widget and its expanded list. My Avatar lists owned avatars, texture sets and
accessories for its Unity texture and thumbnail workflows.

The **My Avatar** page ends after its introduction, animated avatar preview and
Install control. The former “See what it does” link and lower demo sections have
been removed. Its animated background continues behind the footer to the bottom
of the page, without a separate background strip.
