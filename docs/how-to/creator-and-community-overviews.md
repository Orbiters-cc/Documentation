# Use the creator and community overviews

These overviews require the matching application release. They provide shortcuts
to existing tools; the detailed tabs remain available in the page navigation.

## Creator overview

Open **Creator → Overview** for:

- Revenue from the last 30 days, with individual currencies or an explicitly
  labelled converted estimate. Incomplete synchronization and unknown amounts are
  identified. Open Revenues for the full report.
- Your latest assets and **Create**, which opens the same private draft editor as
  the Assets tab. Asset images use the same access rules as the full asset list.
- Your galleries with their five latest images. Use **+** to create a gallery,
  select its Discord room and layout, and choose whether to make it public.
  New galleries start private. Import earlier images from the Galleries tab.
- Supporter tiers and distinct active linked people in each tier. A person may
  appear in more than one tier; disabled or closed accounts are excluded.
- Latest social posts and their delivery or scheduling state.

Each preview loads independently. If a connection fails, retry that preview;
the other sections remain available.

## Community overview

Choose a community, then open **Overview**. Upcoming and planning events link to
their management or voting page. **Create event** uses the selected community's
connected Discord server and VRChat group; complete their setup and permissions
in Events if needed.

The overview also shows staff, appeals waiting for attention, Discord membership,
VRChat group membership and verified 18+ members. Staff and counts are scoped to
the selected community. Only people with the appropriate community permissions
can open its management tools.

Discord member counts come from the connected bot. VRChat counts use the latest
group snapshot and show its age when available. Unavailable counts display a dash.
Verified totals cover known active Discord members and synchronized adult-role
members in VRChat, deduplicated by Orbiters account. They are not a claim that every
external member has linked an Orbiters account. Refresh updates the overview;
refresh the VRChat group separately when its snapshot is stale.
