# Manage Privacy and Shared Content

Open **Account → Privacy & data** to download your information, make a privacy
request, review reports or close your account. Each control expands inside the
page. Downloads are prepared in the background, so you can continue using Orbiters.
On a phone, choose **Privacy & data** from the compact **Account section** selector.

## Download Your Information

1. Expand **Download your data** and select **Prepare export**.
2. Follow progress in **Your requests**. An account notification links to the
   completed export.
3. Select **Download ZIP** within seven days. The authenticated download contains
   structured JSON records, available uploads and shared commission attachments.
   Its manifest explains the included domains and lists unavailable originals.

Credentials and internal security evidence are excluded. For additional personal
information, context or a formal portability assessment, use **Ask about your data**.

## Correct Information or Ask for Removal

Edit your profile directly where possible. For access, portability, correction,
erasure, restriction, objection or a complaint, describe the request and include
the relevant content link. Do not attach passwords or identity documents.

The request shows its status, response and response deadline. GDPR requests normally
receive a response within one calendar month. A necessary extension is explained
within that month and adds at most two months. You can also use the operator email
in the website privacy notice without creating an account.

## Connect, Disconnect or Hide Content

Discord and Telegram connections are managed from **Account → Overview**. Connect
another valid sign-in method before disconnecting your last one. GitHub and Trello
have their existing controls in **Account → Connections**.

A verified connection establishes which imported messages and images belong to you.
You can hide your own content from its discussion or image control. **Hidden content**
in Privacy & data lets you restore it. Reconnecting the same source identity allows
you to recover its hidden-content controls, including after a new website signup.

Hiding on Orbiters does not remove the original from Discord or Telegram. Reimports
keep the website hiding decision. A creator cannot hide somebody else's imported
message merely because it appears under that creator's asset. Report it for staff
review when there is a separate moderation or privacy concern.

## Preserve Commission History

The commission creator controls public hiding through the commission privacy
control. The customer cannot hide the creator's commission. Public hiding preserves
the private record for both participants; it does not cancel the work or payment.
Download the confirmation from the commission page to keep the accepted terms.

## Close Your Account

Expand **Close your account**, read the explanation and confirm the inline checkbox.
If your last sign-in is more than ten minutes old, sign in again and return to the
same section to confirm closure.

Closure removes website membership, sign-in credentials, account connections,
profile information, private settings and private library records. It disables
future account sync and invalidates account access and export downloads.

Shared native discussions remain with a **Deleted account** attribution. Imported
messages keep their external source attribution. Commissions, deliveries and
transaction context remain for the other participants. Content already hidden
stays hidden. Necessary accounting, security and rights-case evidence follows its
retention policy.

Signing up later creates a new membership; it does not reactivate the old account.
To remove particular personal information embedded in shared records, use a specific
erasure request. Those requests are assessed separately from normal content ownership.

## Report or Contest a Decision

Use **Report a problem** on a discussion, listing, profile, image preview or commission.
Provide the reason, enough context to locate the issue, and the good-faith confirmation.
Follow decisions and submit an appeal under **Privacy & data**. A staff restriction
is separate from the author's hiding control. Restoring your own content does not
remove a staff restriction.
