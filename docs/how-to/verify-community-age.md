# Verify your community age status

Orbiters remains fully safe for work. Age verification records whether an accepted
source establishes that you are at least 18. **Not yet verified** does not mean
that you are under 18.

Open **Account → Overview → Age verification** to see your result and sources.
This feature records eligibility; it does not assign VRChat group roles or change
access to meetup instances.

## Link VRChat with a friend request

1. Select **Link VRChat** to start a 15-minute linking session.
2. Open the service account profile linked on the page. Send it a friend request
   from your VRChat account.
3. Select **Check incoming requests**. Recent requests may also appear to other
   members who are linking at the same time.
4. Find your profile and select **It’s me**. Check the profile again, then select
   **Confirm my account**.

You never enter your VRChat password or login code on the member linking page.
Only one Orbiters account can be associated with a given VRChat account. A claimed
request cannot be reused after unlinking. Linking does not automatically accept
or clear the friend request.

If you are already friends with the service account, cannot find your request,
or selected the wrong profile, contact an Orbiters administrator through Discord.
Provide your Orbiters user ID and VRChat profile link. Do not provide passwords
or login codes.

## Understand the result

- **18+ verified:** at least one accepted source is valid.
- **Not yet verified:** no accepted source currently establishes adulthood.
- **Review required:** staff placed the account on review. New evidence does not
  automatically clear this hold.

VRChat must expose its explicit **18+** result. A hidden badge or general age
verification does not establish adulthood for Orbiters. You can refresh the
linked account from this panel.

Other sources include manual staff verification, qualifying Furality convention
evidence reviewed by staff, and a configured adult-verification role in a trusted
Discord server. Each source has its own status. Losing one does not remove an
independent valid source.

## Remove a connection

Select **Unlink VRChat**, then confirm. This withdraws evidence from that VRChat
account while retaining other sources and any review hold. It does not remove a
VRChat friendship.

Age information is included in the account data export. Deleting your Orbiters
account removes its verification records. Orbiters does not provide an identity
document upload field for this workflow.
